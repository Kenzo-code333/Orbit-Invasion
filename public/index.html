    How to play

    1. Drag from one of your planets and release to launch a fleet in a straight line. The label shows flight time to your finger/cursor.
    2. Pick how many ships to send: 25% / 50% / 100% (buttons or keys 1 2 3).
    3. Planets keep orbiting — aim ahead of moving targets. Inner rings move faster.
    4. Landing fleets fight defenders 1-for-1. More attackers = captured. Your own planets get reinforced.
    5. Enemy fleets that touch in space cancel out. You only see an enemy fleet's size when it gets close to one of your planets.
    6. The sun burns any fleet that touches it. Missed shots fly off the map and are lost.
    7. Every 2 minutes a solar flare wipes out all fleets near the sun (10-second warning). After 6 minutes the sun grows and swallows the inner ring.
    8. Win by owning 60% of planets, being the last one standing, or having the most planets after 10 minutes.

    Inner planets: 1 ship/s · Middle: 2/s · Outer: 3/s. Neutral planets don't produce.
